01 / CYBERCRIME

What is Cybercrime?

Cybercrime happens in many ways. It can be a fake text message to get your information, someone stealing your money online or someone getting into your accounts without permission. It can happen to anyone and it's important to know what to watch out for.

⌘

Hacking & Malware

Someone breaks into your online account or puts harmful software on your device to steal your personal information.

◈

Identity Theft & Fraud

Someone uses your name, passwords or banking details and pretends to be you.

◎

Cyberstalking

When you get repeated unwanted messages, online threats or tracking online.

↗

Phishing & Extortion

When you receive messages that try to steal your information and scare you to sending money.

02 / COMMON SCAMS

Common Scams

Scammers usually will pretend be someone you trust such as a company, bank or a friend. Their main goal is to get your money and personal information.

Types of Scams

  • Imposter Scams
  • Phishing & Smishing
  • Online Shopping & Marketplace Fraud
  • Investment & Crypto Scams
  • Romance Scams
  • Job & Employment Scams

⚑ RED FLAGS

Watch out if someone:

  • Pushes and rushes you to make a decision
  • Asks for gift cards, crypto or wire transfers as a payment method
  • Tells you to keep the conversation secret
  • Asks for your passwords or banking codes
  • Contacts you from an unusual number or email such as a private number/no caller id
  • Promises something that sounds too good to be true

03 / AI AND SCAMS

Artificial Intelligence

AI can now be used to create fake texts, photos, voices and videos that seem real and vivid. AI also now allows a voice to sound like someone you know.

◉

Voice Cloning

A caller may sound exactly like someone you know

▣

Deepfake Videos

A video can be taken from the internet and it can be edited to make someone appear a certain way and say something they have never said.

✧

AI-Powered Fraud

Fake profiles, rental ads and investment deals can look real.

Not everything you read or hear is real. If something doesn't feel right, call the person or business yourself to make sure.
