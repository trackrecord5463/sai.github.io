01 / CYBERCRIME

What is Cybercrime?

Cybercrime happens when someone uses a phone, computer or the internet to steal, threaten, trick or harm others. It can be as simple as a fake message or as serious as someone getting into your accounts.

⌘

Hacking & Malware

Someone gets into an account without permission, or installs software that can steal information.

◈

Identity Theft & Fraud

A criminal uses your name, passwords or banking details to pretend to be you.

◎

Cyberstalking

Repeated unwanted messages, threats or tracking online. You do not have to deal with it alone.

↗

Phishing & Extortion

Messages that try to steal your information or scare you into sending money.

02 / COMMON SCAMS

Common Scams

Scammers often pretend to be someone you trust: a bank, delivery company, government agency or even a friend. Their goal is usually to get your money or personal information.

Types of Scams

  • Imposter Scams
  • Phishing & Smishing
  • Online Shopping & Marketplace Fraud
  • Investment & Crypto Scams
  • Romance Scams
  • Job & Employment Scams

⚑ RED FLAGS

Watch out if someone:

  • Rushes you to make a decision
  • Asks for gift cards, crypto or wire transfers
  • Tells you to keep the conversation secret
  • Asks for your passwords or banking codes
  • Contacts you from an unusual number or email
  • Promises something that sounds too good to be true

03 / AI AND SCAMS

Artificial Intelligence

AI can make fake messages, photos, voices and videos look or sound real. That means a familiar face or voice is not always proof that a message is genuine.

◉

Voice Cloning

A caller may sound exactly like someone you know. Hang up and call that person on their usual number.

▣

Deepfake Videos

A video can be edited to make someone appear to say something they never said. Check the source before sharing it.

✧

AI-Powered Fraud

Fake profiles, rental ads and investment offers can look convincing. Check the details before sending money.

Even a well-written message or a realistic voice can be fake. When something feels off, contact the person or business using a number you found yourself.
