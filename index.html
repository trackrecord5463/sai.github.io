01 / UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

What is Cybercrime?

Cybercrime is illegal activity involving computers, smartphones, networks or the internet. It can involve stealing money, compromising information, harassment or damaging systems.

⌘

Hacking & Malware

Unauthorized access and harmful software that steals information or disrupts devices.

◈

Identity Theft & Fraud

Using stolen personal details to impersonate someone or commit financial fraud.

◎

Cyberstalking

Threats, harassment and unwanted monitoring through digital channels.

↗

Phishing & Extortion

Deceptive messages and demands for money or sensitive information.

02 / SPOT THE SIGNS

Common Scams

A scam is a deceptive scheme designed to trick someone into giving up money, information or access to accounts.

Types of Scams

  • Imposter Scams
  • Phishing & Smishing
  • Online Shopping & Marketplace Fraud
  • Investment & Crypto Scams
  • Romance Scams
  • Job & Employment Scams

⚑ RED FLAGS

Be suspicious when someone...

  • Creates pressure or urgency
  • Requests unusual payment methods
  • Demands secrecy
  • Asks for passwords or private details
  • Uses mismatched contact information
  • Makes an offer too good to be true

03 / EMERGING RISKS

Artificial Intelligence

AI allows computer systems to perform tasks associated with human intelligence. Criminals may use AI to create convincing fake voices, images, videos and messages.

◉

Voice Cloning

Fake voices may imitate a loved one or authority figure. Call back using a number you already trust.

▣

Deepfake Videos

Altered video can make someone appear to say or do something they never did.

✧

AI-Powered Fraud

Fake dating profiles, investment pitches, rental listings and online stores may look realistic.

Remember: Robotic speech and poor grammar are not reliable tests for AI. Verify the person and the claim through an independent, trusted channel.
